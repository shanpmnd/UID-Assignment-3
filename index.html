OUR STORY

Big Bang Yogurt began in 2019 with a simple idea: create a place where dessert could be both delicious and feel-good. What started as a frozen yogurt shop quickly became a local favourite for fresh, light, and satisfying treats.

As our community grew, so did our vision. We wanted to offer more than just yogurt — something warm, indulgent, and crafted with the same care.

That's how Her Little Patisserie was born. From the same space, we introduced a range of handcrafted cakes and pastries to sit alongside our signature frozen yogurt.

Whether you're craving something refreshing or something rich and decadent, we're here to make every visit a little sweeter.
